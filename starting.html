Waste segregation guide

Know what goes where.

Sorting waste correctly helps recover useful materials and handle harmful items with care. Local rules can differ, so check your community's collection guidance too.

Biodegradable

Food scraps, fruit peels, leaves, and other compostable organic matter.

COMPOST

Recyclable

Clean paper, cardboard, glass, and accepted metal or plastic containers.

KEEP CLEAN

Residual

Items that cannot be composted or recycled through local services.

CHECK LOCALLY

Special or hazardous

Batteries, bulbs, electronics, and chemicals need dedicated drop-off.

HANDLE WITH CARE

Tip: Empty and rinse containers, keep recyclables dry, and never place batteries or electronics in regular bins.

Recycling information

A better recycle starts before the bin.

Recycling works best when materials are sorted and prepared correctly. Keep these simple checks in mind, then follow the rules for your local collection program.

01Check which materials your local program accepts.
02Empty and rinse containers; keep paper clean and dry.
03Separate materials as required. Do not bag loose recyclables unless asked.
